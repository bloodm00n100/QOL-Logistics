Version: 1.29.4
  Features:
    - Added Lane Sorters that filter one item onto a chosen lane of a single belt.
    - Added Belt Diverters that route filtered items to adjacent belts and balance other items across both lanes ahead.
    - Diverters support parallel belts running either way, outward side belts, and underground belt entrances.
    - Added a tile-based side picker, Alt-mode output arrows, and an optional round-robin mode for sending one item to both sides.
    - Lane Sorters and Belt Diverters match base-game, Space Age, and compatible modded belt tiers.
    - Added an opt-in startup setting to grant Circuit Network and Advanced Combinators.
  Changes:
    - Train Loaders now require Railway and the matching belt loader tier.
  Graphics:
    - Added animated Diverter tread beneath carried items, with a metal housing drawn above them.

Version: 1.28.2
Date: 2026-11-18
  Features:
    - Added Gravel Path. Place ordinary stone directly on land, one stone per tile, without crafting or research.
    - Gravel Path increases walking speed by 20%. Mine the path to recover the stone.
    - Placing Gravel Path clears ground decorations such as stumps and small rocks from the paved tiles.
